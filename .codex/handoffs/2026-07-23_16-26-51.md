### Subtitle fast-path relaxation — reverted

The log category `no line crop` establishes only that crop extraction failed. It does not establish that the rejected component was a full-width subtitle band below the former 14% height threshold.

The added fixtures drew solid white rectangles and mocked a successful recognizer response. They therefore did not demonstrate that real subtitle glyph masks were being rejected, nor that accepting wider 8–14% bands would avoid false-positive crop/recognition work. The threshold relaxation and tests that encoded that unproven premise were removed.

The existing full-OCR fallback, low-confidence rejection, and valid low-confidence-to-fallback regression test remain.
