## Important decisions

- `CustomAIRequestDeadline` is frozen and validates finite numeric values, including overflow paths. Its terminal message is exactly `Custom AI request deadline expired` and carries no provider data.
- All normal and streaming retries reuse one deadline; stream chunks check after read and before parsing/callback, and stream responses close in `finally`.
- Pending requests retain their original snapshot/deadline. A user-triggered profile refresh intentionally discards it and builds a new snapshot from the refresh time.
- Race candidates share the deadline and the coordinator waits at most its remaining duration. Unstarted futures are cancelled and race identities are released during expiry cleanup.
