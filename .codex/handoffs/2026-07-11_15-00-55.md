## Optimization behavior

- Reuses the existing secret-safe, route-isolated, LRU-bounded latency history.
- Requires at least eight observations.
- Chooses `min(configured timeout, max(4.0s, P90 * 4))`.
- Keeps the full configured timeout for sample-poor or slow routes.
- Keeps the full timeout for stream and race modes.
- After a route error, the next request gets one full-time probe; one success
  re-enables the conservative fast-route deadline.
- Adds no retry, hedged/duplicate request, provider switch, or paid API call.
- Freezes the chosen deadline and diagnostic reason in the existing immutable
  request snapshot.
- Validates the snapshot at the worker boundary and caps it at the established
  10.0s maximum.
- Publishes the current timeout seconds/reason through runtime metrics without
  adding a hot-path log.

Requests documentation was checked to confirm that its scalar timeout is a
socket inactivity deadline, not a wall-clock cap for the whole response:
https://requests.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user/quickstart/#timeouts

## Important decisions

- Do not retry a request after waiting for the full read deadline. The subtitle
  is normally stale by then, and retrying would increase cost and provider load.
- Do not hide hedged requests inside safe mode. Explicit race mode already owns
  that trade-off.
- Do not change the slow relay route; its 5--8.5s successful history needs the
  full existing timeout.
- Do not cancel OCR stability Tk timers from the worker thread merely to remove
  about 0.47 stale callbacks per second. The observed benefit is mostly log
  readability and does not justify new cross-thread timer risk.
- Do not loosen translation-cache matching. The latest video subtitles are
  genuinely new, and fuzzy cache reuse could display the wrong translation.
