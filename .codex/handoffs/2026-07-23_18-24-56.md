# Handoff — subtitle fast-path diagnostics review fixes

## Task summary

Reviewed Grok's content-free Subtitle Fast Path diagnostics and corrected its
measurement semantics without changing any OCR selection, recognition, filter,
or fallback behavior.

## Findings and fixes

1. A fast-path failure emits one diagnostic log before full OCR and another after
   full OCR.  The original publisher added the complete per-frame diagnostic map
   at both points, so crop, rejection, recognition, and fast-path counters were
   double-counted whenever a fallback occurred.
2. The outcome selected for a full-fallback log came from the first fast-path
   outcome still present in the map.  A message saying that full OCR produced
   text could therefore be labelled `outcome=fast_path_no_line_crop`.

The publisher now records each counter delta once per frame and each log call
passes its explicit stage outcome.  It retains content-free logs and existing
coalescing.
