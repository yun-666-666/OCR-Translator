# O6 review repair: PaddleOCR prewarm metrics integrity and privacy

## Task summary

Reviewed Grok's O6 observation-only implementation for PaddleOCR prewarm and first-OCR latency. The phase, generation, worker-wait, and diagnostics coverage was structurally sound, but the first review found two defects:

- A completed/failed prewarm snapshot stayed terminal while every later worker wait republished it. This recorded the same prewarm phase and total durations once per OCR frame, corrupting RuntimeMetrics P50/P90/counts.
- The supposedly path-safe settings summary retained the basename of a configured source directory. A custom basename can itself contain user-sensitive project information.

The repair records terminal phase timings once per prewarm generation and changes the source-directory observation to configured/present booleans only. It leaves the background prewarm workflow, engines, 20-second wait cap, and OCR behavior unchanged.

## Important decisions

- `RuntimeMetrics.record_timing()` only receives prewarm phase/total samples on the first terminal publication for each generation. Worker-wait and first-OCR-wait timings remain independent and continue to be sampled at their own boundaries.
- No raw or basename-only source directory is kept in the metrics snapshot, log labels, or diagnostics panel.
- The existing untracked Grok O6 handoff and unrelated untracked workspace files were preserved; this tracked handoff is the review-repair record.
