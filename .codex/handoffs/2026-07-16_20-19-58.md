## Important decisions

- Qt centers `QTextCharFormat` outlines on the glyph edge, so its native pen
  width must be substantially thinner than the user-facing setting.
- The persistent range remains 0 through 6; only the final native pen width is
  scaled by 0.25.
- Color order remains source, target/background, translated text, translated
  outline, mapped row-major into two columns.
