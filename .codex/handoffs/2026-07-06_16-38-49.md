# Task summary

Investigated the latest GUI run for fast English subtitles and reduced the
PaddleOCR realtime bottleneck.

Latest log evidence from `translator_debug.log` around 2026-07-06 16:28-16:29:

- PaddleOCR now uses the subtitle fast path in the GUI.
- 274 PaddleOCR samples averaged 0.124s, with p50 0.063s, p90 0.266s, and max
  0.360s.
- The fast path recognized text 160 times, but it fell back to full OCR 114
  times (`found no line crop` or `found no usable text`).
- Full OCR fallback is the slow path: roughly 0.20s-0.34s in this run.
- The log also showed repeated noisy OCR strings such as `heet or nive yn`,
  `-Whatabint`, and `aLlu er the flbr.`, showing that wrong or overly broad
  crops were sometimes being recognized and submitted before a better subtitle
  frame arrived.
- Translation was also backing up under rapid subtitles: 235 latest-translation
  queue events and several Custom AI translations taking 1.4s-5.9s. That is a
  separate user-visible latency pressure, but the OCR-side cause was frequent
  fallback and noisy crops.

Root cause:

- The subtitle cropper relied too heavily on bright pixels. When the video frame
  had bright background areas or lower-contrast subtitle text, it either chose a
  broad non-subtitle region or failed to find a subtitle crop.
- When crop extraction failed, realtime OCR fell back to the full PaddleOCR
  detector/recognizer pipeline, which is too slow for short-lived subtitles.
- Two-line subtitles were sent to `TextRecognition` one line at a time, adding
  avoidable per-call overhead.

Implemented fix:

- Refactored subtitle crop extraction into a reusable mask-to-line-crop helper.
- Added filters for broad bright background regions and thin full-width scene
  boundaries.
- Added an edge/local-contrast fallback mask when bright-pixel extraction does
  not produce usable subtitle line crops.
- Added center/bottom-biased candidate scoring so subtitle-like crops are
  favored over background visual noise.
- Batched multiple subtitle line crops into one `TextRecognition.predict()` call
  instead of one call per line.

# Important decisions

- Kept full PaddleOCR fallback for now, because it still recovers some frames.
  The goal of this pass is to reduce how often the app reaches that fallback.
- Did not change translation scheduling or Custom AI concurrency in this pass,
  even though the latest log shows translation latency is also a major factor.
