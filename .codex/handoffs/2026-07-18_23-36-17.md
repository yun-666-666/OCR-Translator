# O7 Preview OCR shutdown and Tk-thread safety repair

## Task summary

Repaired the two issues found during the O7 review. A completed Preview OCR future now never falls back to widget work from its worker thread when Tk callback scheduling fails. Application shutdown now invalidates Preview OCR, clears pending work, and shuts down its executor without blocking the UI.

## Important decisions

- A failed `root.after` now discards the completion through worker-safe state only; it never invokes `_finish_preview_ocr_job` directly.
- App close invalidates the Preview generation before executor shutdown, so an already-running OCR task cannot later write a widget.
- `shutdown(wait=False, cancel_futures=True)` cancels queued Preview work without blocking the Tk close path. A running OCR job remains non-cancellable by design, but its completion is safely discarded.
