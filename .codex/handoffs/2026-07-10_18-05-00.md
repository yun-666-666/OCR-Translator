# Capture And OCR Hot-Path Log Optimization

## Task summary

Completed the next major optimization in the ongoing audit: remove successful per-frame capture/OCR debug writes from the critical path while retaining periodic health, slow-event, fallback, state-change, and error evidence.

The exact 15:28:58-15:29:28 real UI session contained 858 selected hot-path events:

- 346 successful captures (344 MSS plus 2 pyautogui startup benchmark captures);
- 342 per-frame capture timings;
- 44 PaddleOCR routing messages;
- 44 PaddleOCR subtitle fast-path successes;
- 44 PaddleOCR timings;
- 19 OCR frame-cache hits;
- 19 OCR cache-hit timings.

An offline replay of this exact ordered session through the new rules emits 49 lines and suppresses 809 repetitions, a 94.3 percent reduction.

## New behavior

- Successful capture messages are coalesced every five seconds per backend and region size.
- Normal capture and OCR timing samples are coalesced every five seconds.
- Slow timing samples use independent one-second channels, so a recent normal sample cannot hide the first slow event.
- Slow thresholds are:
  - MSS capture: 50 ms;
  - pyautogui capture: 250 ms;
  - OCR frame-cache hit: 50 ms;
  - PaddleOCR processing: 500 ms.
- PaddleOCR routing is coalesced every five seconds.
- Successful Paddle subtitle recognition logs only line count and character count; it no longer repeats recognized source text in the debug file.
- Backend failures/fallbacks, source-context changes, unknown-model fallback, Paddle no-text/no-crop fallback, filters, and exceptions remain immediate.
- If the coalescing gate itself fails, `log_debug_coalesced` now falls back to the direct logger and does not change capture/cache/OCR control flow.
